An operator states a production goal, a language model translates it into a ProductionIntent, and a deterministic compiler turns that into an Arrowhead Choreographer plan. Before anything reaches a machine, the plan is checked statically and dry-run on a digital twin of the fourteen workstations; the benchmark oracle then decides whether the plan does what was asked. Pick a scenario and a translation: the certified gold plan or one of the plausible wrong ones (traps).

How this page works. The station models are a JavaScript port of the limits and formulas the cell publishes to the plan generator (tool wear = cycle/60 · 300/Vc, stack height = 144 mm + layers · case height, coating cure windows, payload limits). Execution follows the twin's rules: steps in topological order, the plan repeated session-quantity times, station state carried over, stop at the first failing step. It is an illustration and not the cell's own digital twin. Oven ramp slopes are schematic; only plateau temperatures and dwell are checked. The reviewer (L5) and the human gate (L7) are not modelled here.

Scenarios, gold translations, traps and oracle expectations: intent_benchmark.json v2.0 (X01, X03, X08). Source: github.com/palvarga/arrowhead-workflows.
