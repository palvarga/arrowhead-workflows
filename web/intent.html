Intent to Recipe: shared-tool tightening

Runs of the Intent-to-Recipe pipeline on the forklift example (intent paper, Section 9). A Translator proposes an interpreted intent and a draft Recipe; the deterministic Recipe Validator applies checks I1–I2 and V1–V9; accepted Recipes are compiled to a Choreographer plan, compared with the hand-authored forklift/plan.json, and executed in the kit. See the forklift simulation for the execution itself.

intent text→Translator→draft Recipe→Validator I1–I2, V1–V9→accept / revise / clarify / reject→Compiler → plan.json→kit dry run
