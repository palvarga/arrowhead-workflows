Duck Cell Orders

The expert tier of the benchmark tests one station at a time, one session, one Action. These three intents go beyond it: an order that crosses six stations as a plan of several Actions, with every housing tracked from station to station (O1); two orders that run as concurrent Workflow Sessions and share the AMR through a lease (O2); and an order that has to be resumed from the state the cell reports after a stop (O3). Each comes with a reference plan and plausible wrong translations, played through the same checks: static validation, a digital-twin dry run, and the benchmark oracle.

How this page works. This page is a copy of Duck Cell with an extended model. New here: plans with several Actions (the Choreographer's plan hierarchy), a material-flow check that tracks every housing by station and stage and refuses a step whose parts are not there, the oven-door interlock for the AMR (door opens by hand only at ≤ 60 °C), concurrent sessions run on one time line with nominal durations (AMR 1 m/s plus 1 min handling, CNC cycle times, oven ramp 5 °C/min), a resource-lease service (a new system, not part of the TDK cell) with double-booking and deadlock detection, and a twin that starts from the state the cell reports (O3). Durations, floor coordinates and the lease service are schematic. It is an illustration and not the cell's own digital twin; the reviewer (L5) and the human gate (L7) are not modelled.

Scenarios O1–O3 are written in the format of intent_benchmark.json v2.0, extended with actions, sessions, flow, initial_state, deadline and makespan. Source: github.com/palvarga/arrowhead-workflows (folder duck-orders/).
